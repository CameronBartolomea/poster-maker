A Quick Reference for Real Estate Agents
Put the Three Cs into conversation.
Ask about the positive. Listen for what matters. Explain a relevant difference.
Prospect
“I already have an agent.”
01 Compliment
“That's great. Sounds like you're in good hands.”
02 Compare
“What do they do that you really like?”
Prospect
“She knows the neighborhood really well. Sometimes we have to chase her for updates, though.”
03 Contrast
Example · only if true for you“That makes sense, she sounds great. I'm particular about keeping people posted. Happy to show you what that looks like.”
Finish with a specific practice you actually follow, not a promise you're making up on the spot. If they describe a fully positive experience, don't force a difference. Just leave it at Compliment.
Listen for what matters.
Whatever they bring up in Compare, it usually falls into one of these. Answer the one they actually named.
Communication
If they mention being hard to reach, tell them how you actually keep people posted.
Local Knowledge
If they value expertise, point to a specific way you back that up with real information.
Personal Attention
If they value being a priority, describe how you make clients feel like one, specifically.

If they are happy with their agent, respect that. Do not manufacture dissatisfaction or force a difference. Respect existing representation and follow your brokerage's guidance before discussing next steps. Per NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16.
