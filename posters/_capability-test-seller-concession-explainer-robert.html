Buyer & Agent Education

A seller credit isn't just one thing.

When a seller agrees to a concession, that credit can be split across more than one place at once. Here's where it can go.

Seller Credit

Negotiated as part of the purchase contract, within program and investor limits. Can be divided across any combination of the three below, not just one.

Rate Buydown

Buys down the interest rate for some or all of the loan term, lowering the monthly payment.

Closing Costs

Covers lender and title fees the buyer would otherwise bring to the table at closing.

Prepaid Items

Funds the initial property tax and insurance reserves collected at closing.

A Credit Rarely Goes to Just One Box
AClosing Costs + Prepaid Items
BRate Buydown + Closing Costs
CAll three, split by what the contract allows
About the Numbers

This explains the concept only. How much of a credit can be used, and in which of these ways, depends on the loan program, the lender, and the actual numbers in your contract and that day's pricing.
