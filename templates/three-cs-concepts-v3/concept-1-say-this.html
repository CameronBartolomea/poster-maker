Concept 1 — Not Final
A Quick Reference for Real Estate Agents

Already have an agent? Here's exactly what to say.

Three moves, in order. Say them, don't skip them.
01 · Compliment · Always
“That's great, sounds like you're in good hands.”

Costs nothing. Say it first and mean it, before you ask anything else.

02 · Clarify · Always, Before Anything Else
“Just so I ask the right questions, are you under a signed agreement with them, or is that more of a past relationship?”

This is the only question that matters. The answer tells you exactly what you're allowed to do next.

03 · Contrast · Only If Not Signed
“What's one thing you'd want to go differently this time?”

Let them answer first, then offer one specific, true difference. Nothing more.

STOP IF

They're under a signed, exclusive agreement. Compliment, then step back. Don't offer services. Per NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16 (16-9, 16-13).

Compliment. Clarify. Contrast, only if the door is open.
