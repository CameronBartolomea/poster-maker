Concept 3 — Not Final
When They Say
“I already have an agent.”
Good. That's where you start.
01
Compliment · Always
“That's great, sounds like you're in good hands.”

Costs nothing. Mean it, before you ask anything else.

02
Clarify · Always
“Are you under a signed agreement, or is that someone you've used before?”

The only question that matters. It tells you what you're allowed to do next.

03
Contrast · Only If Not Signed
“What's one thing you'd want to go differently this time?”

Let them answer first, then offer one specific, true difference.

STOP IF

They're under a signed, exclusive agreement. Compliment, then step back. Per NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16 (16-9, 16-13).

Compliment. Clarify. Contrast, only if the door is open.
