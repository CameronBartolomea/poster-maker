A Quick Reference for Real Estate Agents

"I already have an agent."

Three ways to respond well
1

Compliment

Respect the relationship. It costs nothing and it closes no doors.

2

Clarify

Understand where things actually stand before you say anything else.

3

Contrast

Offer a specific point of difference, only once it's clearly welcome.
