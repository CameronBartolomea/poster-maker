If they say they value…
Match the row to what they actually told you during Compare. Never offer a difference that isn't real.
What they valueYour Contrast response
Fast communication“Text me anytime, you'll hear back same day, usually within the hour.”
Feeling kept in the loop“I check in after every showing, even when there's nothing new to report.”
Feeling like a priority“You get my direct line, not a call center. I walk every client through it myself.”
Market or pricing expertise“I'll show you the comps myself and explain exactly how I got there.”
Feeling heard, not sold to“Tell me what you actually need. I'll tell you honestly if I'm the right fit.”

Before any of this: if they're under a signed, exclusive agreement, skip Contrast and respect it. Don't solicit. Per NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16.
