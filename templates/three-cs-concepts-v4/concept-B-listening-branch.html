Concept B — Not Final
A Quick Reference for Real Estate Agents

Someone already has an agent? Don't argue. Listen, then be specific.

Compliment, Compare, Contrast. The whole lesson is in what they say during Compare.
01 · Compliment
“That's great, sounds like you're in good hands.”
Mean it. This is a relationship you're respecting, not an objection you're defeating.
02 · Compare
“What do you like most about working with them?”
You're not fishing for a complaint. You're finding out what actually matters to them.
Kept in the loop
Fast responses
Felt like a priority
03 · Contrast
“I check in after every showing, even when there's nothing new.”
“Text me anytime. You'll hear back same day.”
“You get my direct line, not a call center.”
Match the branch to what they actually said, not a script. Never offer a difference that isn't real.
Exception: if they're under a signed, exclusive agreement, skip Contrast and respect it. Per NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16.
Three moves. One real difference, matched to what they told you.
