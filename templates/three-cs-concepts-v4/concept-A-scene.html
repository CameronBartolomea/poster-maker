Concept A — Not Final
A Quick Reference for Real Estate Agents

One conversation. Three moves. Here's how it actually goes.

Compliment, Compare, Contrast, in order.
01 · Compliment
Respect the relationship first.
02 · Compare
Ask what they value. Listen for it.
03 · Contrast
Answer the specific thing they said.
Why This Works

Compare is the only step that gathers information. Whatever they volunteer, fast replies, feeling informed, a personal touch, that's the one thing your Contrast line has to answer. Don't reach for a different selling point. Respond to what they actually said.

Exception: if they're under a signed, exclusive agreement, skip Contrast and respect it. Per NAR Code of Ethics, Article 16.
