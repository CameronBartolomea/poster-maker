What happens between accepted offer and closing day.

A typical conventional purchase, so you can set your client's expectations before they ask you.

Day 1-3

Application & Disclosures

Buyer signs initial disclosures and uploads income, asset, and ID documents. The clock on required timelines starts here.

Day 3-10

Processing & Verification

Income, assets, and employment are verified directly with the source. This is also when the appraisal is ordered, so it runs in parallel instead of adding time later.

Day 10-18

Appraisal & Underwriting

The appraisal comes back and the file goes to underwriting. Most conditions get issued here. Quick responses from the buyer at this stage keep the file moving.

Day 18-25

Conditions Cleared

Remaining conditions are satisfied and the file is reviewed a final time. Title and escrow prepare closing figures once this step is done.
