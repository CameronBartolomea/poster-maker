The Three Cs
COMPLIMENT·COMPARE·CONTRAST
Start positive. Learn what matters. Show your difference.
01
COMPLIMENTRespect the relationship they already have.
“That's great. Sounds like you're in good hands.”
Said warmly, not defensively
This is the whole step. Not an opener to a pitch, the full move. Mean it, then stop talking.
02
COMPAREAsk what their agent does well. Then listen for both halves of the answer.
“What do they do that you really like?”
“She knows the neighborhood really well.”
Sometimes we have to chase her for updates, though.
The strength. Acknowledge it, don't compete with it.
The opening. This is what Contrast answers.
03
CONTRASTAnswer the specific thing they just told you.
Acknowledge the strength. Then name one specific, true thing you actually do differently.
Example · only if true for you

“That makes sense, she sounds great. I'm particular about keeping people posted. Happy to show you what that looks like.”

The Three Cs. Compliment, Compare, Contrast, in that order.
