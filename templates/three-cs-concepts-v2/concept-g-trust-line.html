A Quick Reference for Real Estate Agents

"I already have an agent."

The Three Cs
01 — COMPLIMENT
Steady. Unhurried.
Mean it. It costs you nothing.
02 — CLARIFY
A real question changes the line.
Ask the one question that matters.
03 — CONTRAST
Earned, not forced.
Offer one real difference. Nothing more.
Acknowledge. Ask. Offer perspective when it's welcome.
